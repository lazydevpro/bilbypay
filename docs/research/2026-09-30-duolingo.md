# Duolingo research for BilbyPay

Research checked 30 September 2026. Primary sources only. This is a design research brief, not an audit of a logged-in Duolingo installation. Duolingo experiments and availability vary by platform/cohort; its old `design.duolingo.com` brand pages now redirect to the design blog hub. Historical design articles are used for principles, while the recent core-tab refresh and 2025 product recap establish the more recent direction.

## The useful idea

Bilby should become a companion whose expressions explain what is happening, celebrate meaningful progress, and make money feel approachable. A repeated static mascot above ordinary forms will not produce that feeling. Give Bilby a consistent visual world, a small vocabulary of useful reactions, and a relationship to the user's own goals.

The strongest transfer is **character + clear feedback + meaningful progress + polished craft**. The objective for a payment app is confidence and successful money tasks; increasing time in the app or spending frequency is not a useful equivalent of learning more.

## What the sources establish

| Evidence | Implication for BilbyPay |
| --- | --- |
| Duolingo built its character family from Duo's simple geometry, recognizable silhouette, large eyes, and shared shape language. Characters appear inside exercises and react to correct answers. [Building character](https://blog.duolingo.com/building-character/) | Build assets around the existing Bilby anatomy and personality. Attach reactions to actual task states. Keep the character recognizable at both a small status size and a large welcome scene. |
| The art direction uses a small number of readable vector shapes, clear silhouettes, negative space, and deliberate exaggeration. The team says extra detail can obscure meaning on small screens. [Shape language](https://blog.duolingo.com/shape-language-duolingos-art-style/) | Simplify Bilby's props and scenes. One obvious visual idea per state: envelope for a gift, growing plant for a goal, protective gesture for account security. Avoid elaborate backgrounds behind money details. |
| Duolingo's recent core-tab refresh addressed inconsistent typography, spacing, and headers. It explicitly rejected artwork everywhere when it lacked purpose. Its system uses tiered header sizes, consistently positioned titles, fewer type styles, and whitespace instead of unnecessary containers. [Core tabs redesign](https://blog.duolingo.com/core-tabs-redesign/) | Personality and restraint can coexist. Home and achievements can be expressive; receipts, review screens, and settings need focused hierarchy. Define the design system before adding more decorative cards. |
| Rive state machines let Duolingo blend character poses, idle motion, facial reactions, and speech states from reusable assets; this supports Android, iOS, and web without sending movies for each response. [World character animation](https://blog.duolingo.com/world-character-visemes/) | Specify a Bilby state machine first. Rive is a candidate, not an automatic dependency decision. A lightweight existing animation stack can implement the first version if it preserves those states and transitions. |
| Streak milestones were redesigned around a visible transformation of Duo, stronger animation timing, and attractive share cards. Duolingo investigated whether its fire metaphor was understood across cultures. [Streak milestone design](https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation/) | Celebrate a user's achieved goal through a Bilby scene or accessory. Make progress understandable in text as well as art. Share cards should be optional and omit balances, recipients, and payment amounts by default. |
| The widget uses a tiny amount of information plus many mascot moods. Its deliberately escalating desperation reflects whether a lesson is unfinished near midnight. [Widget design](https://blog.duolingo.com/widget-feature/) | Borrow contextual character variation and limited information density. Do not make Bilby distressed because someone has not spent, transferred money, saved more, or opened the app. |
| Friend Streak supports accountability while each person acts independently. Duolingo reports a 22% association with daily lesson completion for users with a Friend Streak; this is a company-reported result in learning, not proof of an effect in payments. [Friend Streak product lessons](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-lessons-friend-streak/) | Shared money goals could eventually offer quiet encouragement, with explicit consent and no public wealth comparison. First validate the individual loop. Social features bring privacy, permissions, and backend scope. |
| Friends Quests give people a shared objective and a reward. The 2025 recap adds matching for people without existing friends and stronger rewards for completing Daily Quests. [Friends Quests](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-quests/), [2025 product highlights](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/) | Optional, finite tasks are transferable. Random social pairing is a poor initial fit for private financial activity. Use setup and organization tasks before considering shared goals. |
| Duolingo replaced a raw engagement focus with Time Spent Learning Well. It reworked quest and XP incentives after observing people grind easy activities for points. [Time Spent Learning Well](https://blog.duolingo.com/time-spent-learning-well/) | Reward the outcome that helps the user. Useful candidates are securing an account, setting a chosen savings goal, completing a personal review, or reaching the goal. Never reward repeated transfers, higher spend, or holding more money solely for points. |
| Duolingo says its former Hearts system was particularly discouraging for beginners, who were more likely to exhaust them. Its newer Energy system remained under active testing in the article. [Duolingo Energy](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/) | Do not copy lives, energy, countdowns, or failure penalties into access to money. Payment errors should preserve the draft and explain the next step. |
| In Japan, less intimidating wording increased placement-test uptake. In India, everyday mixed-language terms improved onboarding, and the team removed an early subscription promotion in one experiment. [Research in Asia](https://blog.duolingo.com/lessons-from-asia-turning-local-research-into-global-experiments/), [Duolingo in India](https://blog.duolingo.com/language-learning-for-the-next-billion-duolingo-in-india/) | Onboarding should use familiar payment language and progressive requests. Ask only for what is needed to deliver the next useful action. Test comprehension in the target market, especially local currencies and transfer terminology. |
| Duolingo improved Android startup by deferring nonessential work, including quest/leaderboard fetches. It also rendered lesson completion while preparing later screens. [Android performance](https://blog.duolingo.com/android-app-performance/) | Load the account and primary payment actions before decorative or reward data. Unlike lesson UI, a payment success state must wait for authoritative confirmation. Bilby can show a truthful pending state while processing. |

## Proposed Bilby behavior system

These are recommendations, not features observed in Duolingo or currently confirmed in BilbyPay.

| State | Character role | Example copy / behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Warm introduction; establishes personality | “Money, with a little more joy.” Bilby waves once, then settles. |
| Home | Calm companion in a small living scene | Greets the person without making their balance a game score. One optional goal or useful next step. |
| Choosing a person | Helpful attention | Bilby's gaze follows the selected person or points toward the next action. Recipient identity remains prominent. |
| Entering an amount | Quiet, attentive | Small idle presence. No escalating excitement when the amount increases. |
| Reviewing a payment | Reassuring and still | Amount, recipient, fees, arrival, and funding source get the full visual priority. |
| Processing | Busy but calm | A truthful “Sending…” state. No fake progress percentage or premature celebration. |
| Confirmed | Brief delight | A hop, envelope delivery, or high five plus the exact payment status. The receipt is available immediately. |
| Pending or failed | Helpful support | “This is taking longer than usual” or “Your money hasn't moved” only when verified. Bilby should not look ashamed, angry, or devastated. |
| Savings goal | Builds toward the user's intention | Burrow scenery grows at meaningful milestones. Withdrawals never visibly damage it or punish the character. |
| Gift | Expressive presentation | A small unpacking or ribbon moment after the real gift is issued. Clear redemption information remains accessible. |

Use the mascot as a performer with a few clear rules: optimistic, a little mischievous, proud of the user, and never emotionally dependent on their spending. Motion can react to input and verified outcomes; looping activity should be quiet and limited. Haptic patterns are a **Bilby recommendation**, not a verified Duolingo implementation detail from this research: light selection feedback, a distinct confirmed-success pulse, and restrained error feedback, with device/accessibility preferences respected.

## A suitable first gamification loop

1. A person chooses something useful: send to someone, set up a savings goal, or prepare a gift.
2. Bilby guides that task with one next action and visible progress when the task actually has steps.
3. The app confirms the real result and gives a brief, specific celebration.
4. A voluntary journey records meaningful milestones and unlocks cosmetic Bilby accessories or small scenes. This can be sparse; no invented daily chore is required.
5. The person returns because payments are easy and the product is pleasant. Optional weekly money organization can be tested later if it provides genuine value.

Initial milestones can include account protected, first chosen goal created, first successful payment, first received gift, and a chosen goal achieved. Completion badges should not imply investment performance, better credit, or guaranteed financial benefit. Cosmetic rewards should have no cash value and no token vocabulary.

Do not launch the full set of levels, XP, points, streaks, badges, quests, leaderboards, and a rewards store together. One understandable progress system is enough to learn whether users care. A “send every day” streak, spending leaderboard, chance-based cash reward, countdown to deposit, or sad character after withdrawal would incentivize the wrong behavior.

## Practical acceptance criteria for a design prototype

- Can a new user describe BilbyPay as a payment app after the welcome screen?
- Can they find Send and understand the recipient, total charge, fees, and arrival without interacting with a game element?
- Does every visible Bilby expression correspond to a understandable moment, rather than merely fill blank space?
- Can the primary task finish without watching the entire celebration?
- Are confirmed, pending, failed, cancelled, and reversed states visually and verbally distinct?
- Does reduced motion still communicate all status and progress?
- Do rewards work without requiring more spending or exposing financial activity to friends?
- Does the app stay responsive on the intended Android phone when the mascot is animated?

Primary validation should measure payment task completion, recipient/amount/fee comprehension, time to complete, support/error recovery, and perceived trust. Delight and voluntary return are secondary signals. The appropriate principle from Duolingo is to align the game with the product's useful outcome.
