# CRED, Wise and PayPal: patterns for BilbyPay

Research date: 30 September 2026. Scope: primary public product pages, help documentation, official design publications and CRED's open-source UI libraries. No authenticated competitor app was accessed. Product pages establish supported patterns; they do not establish the exact current screen layout for every country, account or app version. Historical design articles are identified below.

## What the sources establish

| Product | Verified observation | Transferable idea for BilbyPay (our interpretation) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRED Pay | The public product page connects scan/pay to cashback and collectibles. Scanner skins and collectible flairs let a member personalize the payment surface. It also promotes time-limited shopping boosts. [Official product page](https://cred.club/cred-pay) | Make Bilby's world and accessories collectible. Personalization can give repeat use a visible history without adding a second monetary balance. Keep promotional rewards outside the essential payment steps. |
| CRED NeoPOP | CRED's public UI libraries document raised controls, configurable depth, pressed states, tilted/floating buttons and haptic callbacks. The web library is designed around mobile views and motion. These are published design-system examples, not proof of the complete 2026 app. [Flutter library](https://github.com/CRED-CLUB/neopop-flutter), [web library](https://github.com/CRED-CLUB/neopop-web) | Borrow physical feedback: a decisive press, a small depth shift and a short arrival/reveal. Build original rounded Bilby components rather than importing CRED's styling or adding soft shadows everywhere. |
| CRED brand | CRED describes its identity through craftsmanship, distinctive illustration, strong typography and composition. It emphasizes deliberate treatment of its brand assets. [Official brand page](https://newsroom.cred.club/brand/) | Premium should come from consistent art direction and carefully composed moments. A mascot can occupy a polished scene; the rest of the screen needs restraint and clear hierarchy. |

[Send-money guide](https://wise.com/help/articles/2977959/how-do-i-send-money) | Present people and outcomes first. [Transfer-status guide](https://wise.com/help/articles/2452305/how-do-i-check-my-transfers-status) | Give every payment a human-readable timeline and a useful next action. Bilby may carry an envelope, but the authoritative text must state whether money is processing, waiting to be accepted, delivered, failed or returned. |
| Wise visual accessibility | The Wise design team's 2023 article documents simpler icon metaphors, readable type, stronger field boundaries, conspicuous focus treatment, reduced clutter, generous spacing and screen-reader work. This is historical design rationale. [Wise Design article](https://medium.com/transferwise-design/accessible-but-never-boring-part-2-e188db299bd0) | Warm, expressive art can coexist with high-contrast controls and readable financial numbers. | PayPal sending | Official help starts with familiar recipient identifiers and proceeds through amount, payment method and review. A recipient without an account gets guidance to join. Show expiry and cancellation truthfully, based on BilbyPay's implemented rules; do not copy PayPal's 10-day policy. |

## Recommended synthesis

Bilby should make money feel approachable and memorable. The payment contract must remain easy to inspect. Use rich character scenes on welcome, home, savings, gifts and completion; use calm layouts for recipient selection, amount entry, confirmation, problems and receipts. A small contextual Bilby can still connect these quieter pages to the same world.

Choose a single visual language: rounded character art, warm light surfaces, deep ink text, a restrained brand accent and controls with subtle physical depth. CRED-like craft is transferable; its status-driven exclusivity and full dark presentation are not necessary to achieve that craft. Wise shows why distinctive branding need not reduce clarity.

Use motion to explain a state transition: Bilby receives an envelope, places a saved amount into its burrow, or reveals a gift. Keep amounts and actions stable while this happens. Let users continue without waiting for a celebration, and provide an equivalent reduced-motion result.

## Reward rules proposed for BilbyPay

- Reward useful setup and sustained, user-chosen habits: completing account security, reaching a self-set savings milestone, or reviewing a monthly summary. Avoid rewarding transfer volume or spending more.
- Prefer deterministic cosmetic progress: a new room detail, accessory, stamp or expression. Explain the unlock condition and show what is earned.
- Avoid daily payment streaks, money leaderboards, punishment for missing days, cash-dependent luck wheels and countdowns that pressure spending.
- Keep financial value and cosmetic progress visually distinct. Never make an in-app point look like spendable money.
- Keep the receipt visible after success; an optional reward reveal can sit beneath it. A pending payment must not show a delivered celebration.

These are proposed product choices, not claims that competitor apps follow them. CRED's public hourly-cashback promotion explicitly says more transactions increase winning chances; BilbyPay should not transplant that incentive. [Official promotion rules](https://cred.club/cred-pay/winners)

## Payment-first vocabulary and state contract

Use **Balance**, **Add money**, **Send**, **Receive**, **Save**, **Payment**, **Fee**, **Available**, **Processing**, **Arrived**, **Receipt**, **Reference** and named people. Never expose wallet addresses, network names, token tickers, gas, signing, approval allowances, transaction hashes or explorer links in consumer flows. Give support a short BilbyPay payment reference that maps to internal records.

Translate implementation states into real customer outcomes, including exceptions. Show what the user must do and when their money is available. A generic reassuring message cannot replace a failure reason, a displayed fee, a delayed-arrival estimate or the actual behavior of a savings product. Words such as instant, free, safe and guaranteed require corresponding product behavior.

## Validation before visual rollout

For representative send, claim and save journeys, ask whether a new user can identify the recipient, amount received, total charged, timing and next action without assistance. Confirm that they describe BilbyPay as a payment app. Separately test whether Bilby makes the experience more pleasant and understandable. Reward engagement should be measured alongside task completion, mistaken payments, support demand and accessibility, not by session length alone.
