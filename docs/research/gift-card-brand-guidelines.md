# Gift-card artwork and merchant brand guidelines

Research date: **30 September 2026**. Scope: public, official merchant pages, asset kits, brand guidelines and planned provider documentation. This records source provenance and published requirements; it does not establish that BilbyPay has a merchant relationship, reseller agreement or permission to use a particular asset commercially.

## Use in BilbyPay

Merchant artwork selected from these sources is for the **demo preview**. That designation does not itself grant trademark or copyright permission. Do not describe the merchants as BilbyPay partners or describe a public image as approved for BilbyPay.

For the production catalog, prefer the actual provider-returned `artworkUrl` for the relevant product, region and denomination, subject to the provider's distribution agreement and merchant requirements. Keep the provider artwork separate from any curated demo asset. Do not replace a real product's artwork with an invented merchant card design.

The implemented renderer preserves the supplied image, aspect ratio and colors with `resizeMode="contain"`; places BilbyPay's amount, availability, selection mark, occasion and Bilby outside the merchant artwork; and reserves layout space while loading. Broken images fall back to an existing provider logo, then a neutral gift symbol and merchant name. There is no logo tracing, image crop, rotation, recoloring or overlay. This is a conservative implementation choice, not a claim that all merchants publish identical rules. Apply each merchant's more specific requirements where applicable.

## Provider findings and implementation

- **Laso (demo integration):** the [official OpenAPI](https://github.com/LasoFinance/docs/blob/main/api-reference/openapi.json) exposes `GiftCardProduct.product_image_url`, described as a gift card image. The adapter now forwards it as optional `artworkUrl`, retaining the existing `logoUrl` for compatibility. The catalog needs a Bearer token; no live authenticated catalog or purchase was attempted.
- **Bitrefill Business (planned production):** [official product documentation](https://docs.bitrefill.com/reference/get_products) documents `https://cdn.bitrefill.com/primg/{opts}/{product.id}.{webp|png}`. The adapter uses the exact encoded product ID, `w600h400` for artwork, and a separate `w250h100i1` icon URL (`i1` means prefer icon). Some products supply logo-on-background images rather than illustrated card faces; display what the provider supplies.
- Artwork now travels through catalog → gift claim → owned card. A nullable database column keeps the original artwork with an order; old records still use their existing logo. This does not change the purchasing endpoint or checkout rules.
- Production asset use follows the Business distribution agreement; the public image documentation is not evidence that BilbyPay has completed that agreement. [Bitrefill Business API](https://docs.bitrefill.com/docs/Common%20Use%20Cases), [terms](https://www.bitrefill.com/terms/?hl=en).
- Spotify's [current US support page](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/gift-cards/) states gift cards are no longer sold. The app's demo entry was replaced with Amazon.com using verified Bitrefill product artwork. Live catalog availability remains provider-controlled. Other demo amounts/fees remain illustrative; Airbnb remains unavailable in the app demo.
