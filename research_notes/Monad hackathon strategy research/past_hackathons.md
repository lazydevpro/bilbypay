# Monad Past Hackathons, Pitch Competitions & Builder Programs (2024 – Sept 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-24. Many winner lists are only on X/Discord, which search engines index poorly. Gaps are marked explicitly.

## Which hackathons/competitions has Monad Foundation run or sponsored, when, and with what tracks/themes?

### Takeaway
From Oct 2024 to Sept 2026, Monad has run four kinds of events. (1) **Monad Madness** VC pitch competitions for teams that already have an MVP (NYC, Seoul and Bangkok in 2024; Hong Kong in Apr 2025). (2) Flagship online or hybrid hackathons: **evm/accathon** (Feb–Mar 2025, $100K), **Moltiverse** (Feb 2026, $200K, AI agents) and **Metropolis** (Sept 1 – Oct 13, 2026, $250K+, four tracks, **still running**). (3) Regional hackathons such as **Rebel in Paradise** (China, AI, $40K). (4) Community formats: **Monad Blitz**, one-day local hackathons with 50+ editions since May 2025, and the **Missions** online challenges. It has also sponsored ETHGlobal events. The themes moved from general and consumer (2024–25) toward AI agents (2026), and Metropolis now spreads across finance, consumer/payments, social and trust/identity/AI.

### Cited Findings

**Monad Madness (pitch competition series, 2024–2025)**
- Announced Aug 26, 2024 as a "$1M pitch competition." Session One was NYC, Oct 2–4 (2024); Session Two was Seoul, Dec 6–8 (2024). It targets early-to-intermediate teams that have an MVP, may have raised pre-seed, and are raising more. It advertised judges from top VCs, 60+ VCs watching, and $60M of stated investment interest. Teams get pitch workshops and a VC networking event. — [Monad announcement](https://www.monad.xyz/announcements/monads-1m-pitch-competition-monad-madness); [madness.monad.xyz](https://madness.monad.xyz/)
- **Bangkok** edition: Nov 16–17, 2024, immediately after Devcon, co-held with Paradigm. It advertised $500K in prizes. — [Bitget/BlockBeats](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604366660); [DoraHacks page](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/monadmadness/detail)
- **Hong Kong** edition: Apr 9, 2025 at the Hong Kong Web3 Festival, with a reported HK$2M prize pool and 16 shortlisted teams. — [PANews](https://panews.io/articles/y390l6bi); [madness.monad.xyz HK 2025](https://madness.monad.xyz/madness-events/madness-hong-kong-2025)
- A March 2026 Monad blog post, as reported in a search summary, lists Madness as held in NYC, Seoul, Bangkok and Hong Kong with prize pools totaling $260K. That **conflicts** with the "$1M" and "$500K" headline figures; the headline numbers may have included non-cash or investment components. — [Monad blog "Home for Builders"](https://www.monad.xyz/blog/home-for-builders) (the direct fetch returned 404, so this is unverified); [Monad announcement](https://www.monad.xyz/announcements/monads-1m-pitch-competition-monad-madness)

**evm/accathon (Monad's first hackathon, 2025)**
- Hybrid format: an in-person hacker house at ETHDenver on Feb 24–25, 2025, then online Feb 26 – Mar 12, 2025. Applications closed Feb 11, 2025 and entry was selective: "a limited number of submissions will be accepted." Prizes totaled $100K. Winners got a Monad Builder Residency in NYC (capped at $10K per team). Every participating team was eligible for up to $30K in milestone-based funding from the Monad Foundation. — [Monad Substack](https://monadxyz.substack.com/p/the-first-monad-hackathon-evmaccathon); [Monad announcement](https://www.monad.xyz/announcements/the-first-monad-hackathon-evm-accathon)
- Sponsor tracks included Envio (indexing). — [Envio blog](https://docs.envio.dev/blog/announcing-the-monad-envio-hackathon-winners)
- Mentors were announced in waves; the LinkedIn post "Wave 2 of the mentors" confirms at least two waves. — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/monad-foundation_introducing-wave-2-of-the-mentors-for-monads-activity-7295195577036718081-gQvi)

**Monad Blitz (one-day local hackathons, May 2025 onward)**
- More than 50 editions worldwide since launching in May 2025. Winners are peer-voted ("Winners are decided entirely by the people building alongside you"). A testnet event platform, blitz.devnads.com, handles token claims, project submission, team forming and voting. — [Luma ETHDenver 2026](https://luma.com/monad-blitz-ethdenver-2026); [devnads.com](https://devnads.com/)
- ETHDenver edition: Feb 17, 2026, at the Temple nightclub, co-hosted with PizzaDAO (which offered a separate pizza prize). About $10K went to winners. — [Luma](https://luma.com/monad-blitz-ethdenver-2026); [Monad Pulse #016](https://themonadpulse.substack.com/p/the-monad-pulse-016)
- Other 2026 editions: Yogyakarta (Apr 25, 2026), NYC at ETHConf (June 2026), Amsterdam (Aug 2026), and Jakarta (Aug 8, 2026, $2,000 prize pool). Pune ran in Dec 2025. — [UGM news](https://jteti.ugm.ac.id/2026/05/06/mahasiswa-dteti-dominasi-monad-blitz-hackathon-2026-melalui-ugm-blockchain-club/); [Luma NYC](https://luma.com/blitz-nyc-june-2026); [Luma Amsterdam](https://luma.com/blitz-ams-aug-2026); [Luma Pune](https://luma.com/blitz-pune-dec-2025); [monadblitz.world](https://monadblitz.world/)

**Missions (online dev challenges, 2025)**
- "Mission 7: Monad Game Jam" was announced around Aug 14, 2025. Games had to integrate **Monad Games ID**, a cross-game identity with a global leaderboard. A sample entry is Kingdom Survival, a Phaser tower-defense game that submits scores to the Games ID contract using a Privy Global Wallet; nothing found says whether it won. — [Hey post](https://hey.xyz/posts/3cn5amryg83kyycrz5r); [GitHub kingdom-survival](https://github.com/w1th0ut/kingdom-survival); [Monad Games ID](https://monad-games-id-site.vercel.app/)
- The monad-developers GitHub has a "CN Mission 3: MCP Werewolf" repo (an MCP/AI-agent Werewolf game, apparently for the Chinese-language community) and a Farcaster mini-app template, which may be tied to an earlier mission. — [GitHub monad-developers](https://github.com/monad-developers)

**Moltiverse (Feb 2026, AI agents)**
- Hosted by Nad.fun and Monad, with support from AUSD, Paradigm, Dragonfly and AttentionX AI (plus Circle, EF and Thirdweb per TechFlow). It ran Feb 2–15, 2026, and ended Feb 18. Prizes totaled $200K. The **Agent+Token track** ($140K) had 10 winners at $10K each plus a $40K liquidity boost for the highest-market-cap token on nad.fun. The **Agent track** ($60K) had 3 open winners and 3 bounty winners at $10K each. Judging was rolling, with at most 16 winners. — [moltiverse.dev](https://moltiverse.dev/); [Monad X](https://x.com/monad/status/2018354399010042242); [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/newsletter/113221)
- Monad's suggested ideas: worlds for other agents to join, agents that wager or play games, agent-to-agent transactions, and robotics. — [Monad X](https://x.com/monad/status/2018710997716894162?lang=en)

**Rebel in Paradise (China, AI, Jan–Mar 2026)**
- Ran Jan 19 – Feb 28, 2026 in a hybrid format, with hacker camps in Beijing and Shenzhen and about a month of mentorship. Prizes were about $40K ($20K cash plus $20K in credits and resources). Tracks: Agent-native Payments, Intelligent Markets, and Agent-powered Applications. Results were announced around Mar 21, 2026. — [Everstake](https://everstake.com/resources/blog/monad-ignites-the-builder-economy-hackathons-ai-and-a-3-month-accelerator); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/monad-ai-hackathon-concludes-with-11-winning-projects-and-major-llm-partnerships)
- Partners: Kimi, Zhipu AI, DouBao, StepFun, SiliconFlow, YouWare and NVIDIA (special award). VCs: Delphi, Pantera, CoinFund, Vertex and Enlight. — [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/monad-ai-hackathon-concludes-with-11-winning-projects-and-major-llm-partnerships)

**Nitro Accelerator (2026, not a hackathon but part of the builder funnel)**
- Three months (one in NYC, two online, ending in a Demo Day). 15 teams, $500K in VC funding, applications due Mar 14, 2026. — [Everstake](https://everstake.com/resources/blog/monad-ignites-the-builder-economy-hackathons-ai-and-a-3-month-accelerator)

**Metropolis (flagship online hackathon, Sept–Nov 2026, IN PROGRESS)**
- Build window Sept 1 – Oct 13, 2026 (six weeks). Judging Oct 14–27; winners announced Nov 3, 2026. Prize pool $250K+: $30K per track (split among 3 teams) across 4 tracks, a $25K Grand Champion, and sponsor bounties. Runs on Monad mainnet (chain ID 143). — [Metropolis page](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis); [hackathon.monad.xyz](https://hackathon.monad.xyz/)
- Tracks: (1) Onchain Finance & Trading; (2) Consumer Products & Payments ("instant, programmable, and invisible to the end user"); (3) Social, Attention & Culture; (4) Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure (identity, provenance, data ownership, agent trust). — [Metropolis page](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- Rules: open globally to teams and solo builders; existing projects are allowed if the new work is done during the window. Submissions need a working product with a demo, a write-up and a code link. Open source is encouraged but not required. — [Metropolis page](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- Sponsors include ack3, Agora, Alchemy, Chainlink, Dynamic, Envio, Kuru, Privy, Quicknode, Zerion, Nansen, Alibaba Cloud and Kimi, among 13+ others. — [Metropolis page](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)

**Third-party events with Monad sponsorship or tracks**
- 1inch × ETHGlobal "Unite DeFi" (2025): Monad was one of the "further chains" in the Fusion+ cross-chain swap extension challenge (prizes $7.5K / $5K / $3K / $2.5K / $2K; winners announced Aug 6, 2025). — [1inch blog](https://1inch.com/blog/post/unite-defi-1inch-ethglobal-hackathon)
- Monad Foundation is listed as a sponsor of ETHGlobal Buenos Aires (Nov 2025) and ETHGlobal New York 2026. — [ETHGlobal Buenos Aires](https://ethglobal.com/events/buenosaires/prizes); [ETHGlobal NY 2026](https://ethglobal.com/events/newyork2026)
- "Spark" is a BuildAnything hackathon where submissions are a contract address deployed on Monad mainnet or testnet. It is not confirmed as Monad-run, and no winners were found. — [BuildAnything Spark](https://buildanything.so/hackathons/spark)
- MOST (Monad Open Source Track): an ongoing @monad_dev program that gives AI credits to builders who keep shipping open-source projects on Monad. — [devnads](https://devnads.com/)

### Inferences
- The events form a clear funnel: Blitz (one day, local, peer-voted) → Missions and online hackathons → Madness pitch or Nitro accelerator (VC-facing). Hackathon prizes are small next to the post-event funding offered (evm/accathon's $30K milestone grants, Nitro's $500K).
- The theme shift is strong. 2024 Madness winners leaned consumer, gaming and DeFi. Since late 2025, AI agents dominate (Moltiverse, Rebel in Paradise, Metropolis's AI/trust track).

### Gaps
- No detailed pages were found for Madness Seoul, and nothing named an event "Blitz Pro" or "Monad Mission" beyond Mission 7 and CN Mission 3.
- The full Blitz edition list and per-city winners are not indexed; I found only the Yogyakarta winners.
- ETHGlobal Monad prize winners were not retrieved. The showcase can be filtered by the Monad prize at https://ethglobal.com/showcase.

## Which projects won and what did each build? Any patterns?

### Takeaway
Confirmed winners span consumer and social apps, sports and betting, games, DeFi, and a growing share of AI agents and trading terminals. AI-plus-trading shows up again and again: KiSignals, Gorillionaire, OpenAlice, AMMO.

### Cited Findings

**Monad Madness NYC (Oct 2024)**
- 1st: **EarnOS**, a web3 ad and rewards platform with chain abstraction and email login.
- 2nd: **Sauce.GG**, a memecoin trading platform across Solana, ETH and Base.
- 3rd: **LEVR**, on-chain investing with up to 5x leverage combined with sports entertainment.
- Community awards: **Hyperplex** (AMM LP restaking and yield), **Plato** (gamified restaurant-spend rewards) and **Symphony** (cross-chain DeFi trading via chain abstraction).
- Source for all of the above: [Gate Learn](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/monad-madness-nyc-startup-competition-winners-announced-six-innovative-projects-showcased/4376); [AiCoin](https://www.aicoin.com/en/article/422609)

**Monad Madness Bangkok (Nov 2024, with Paradigm)**
- **RareBetSports**: on-chain daily fantasy sports and sports betting across 10+ sports, paid in USDC.
- **Kizzy**: a mobile web3 social and competitive entertainment app connecting users with creators.
- **Sparkball**: a 4v4 MOBA crossed with a ball-sports game.
- **Dusted**: a tokenized social protocol.
- **Pulse**: a wearable health device that monetizes health data on-chain.
- **Mozi**: PvPfi, combining player-vs-player mechanics with LST yield.
- **Jigsaw**: a CDP stablecoin (jUSD).
- Source for all of the above: [Bitget/BlockBeats](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604366660); [BlockBeats](https://m.theblockbeats.info/en/news/55875)
- Placement caveat: the ranking of RareBetSports 1st, Kizzy 2nd and Sparkball 3rd (the rest as community awards) comes from an LLM summary of the Bitget article and should be verified. One writeup summarized the cohort as centering on gaming and social. — [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604366660)

**Monad Madness Hong Kong (Apr 9, 2025)**
- 1st: **AMMO**, a "Massively Online Multi-Agent Architecture."
- 2nd: **GM**, an AI-for-longevity app (listed as "GM Network" elsewhere).
- 3rd: **TypeX**, a keyboard with embedded transactions.
- Most Popular Community: **Morpheus Protocol / Morpheus Farm**.
- Most Popular Team: **LIKWID**.
- Perks: VC introductions, Monad ecosystem support, and a fast track into the IOSG Kickstarter program.
- Source for all of the above: [PANews](https://panews.io/articles/y390l6bi); [AiCoin](https://www.aicoin.com/en/article/453532)

**evm/accathon (Feb–Mar 2025)**
- **KiSignals**: a terminal aggregating sentiment, tickers and information from Telegram and Twitter.
- **StageFun**: crowdfunded events that repay funders from ticket and sponsorship revenue (one newsletter misspells it "StakeFun").
- **OwnPay**: RFID-based payments.
- **Gorillionaire**: an AI trading terminal with buy/sell signals from on-chain data, whale activity and listings. It uses Envio indexing and also won Envio's sponsor track.
- **StitchAI**: a cross-platform memory and knowledge layer for AI agents.
- Source for all of the above: [Nadsletter #4](https://medium.com/@monadwhisper/nadsletter-issue-4-monad-updates-as-of-april-2025-17a31f89a2b1); [Envio blog](https://docs.envio.dev/blog/announcing-the-monad-envio-hackathon-winners); [ASXN Monad Memo](https://x.com/asxn_r/status/1909651503557808447)

**Moltiverse (Feb 2026)**
- More than 400 projects entered and 16 winners were announced, described as "agent VCs, dating apps, open worlds." The individual names are in an X thread that could not be fetched (HTTP 402). — [Monad X](https://x.com/monad/status/2026363272249655540)
- Notable submissions (not confirmed winners): rock-paper-scissors, battle-royale, survival and word-guessing agent games. — [Monad Pulse #016](https://themonadpulse.substack.com/p/the-monad-pulse-016)

**Rebel in Paradise (Mar 21, 2026)**

**Metropolis (2026)**
- No winners yet; the announcement is due Nov 3, 2026. — [Metropolis](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)

### Inferences
- Patterns: (a) consumer, social and entertainment, including sports and betting (RareBet, LEVR, Kizzy, Plato, StageFun); (b) trading terminals and AI trading agents (KiSignals, Gorillionaire, OpenAlice, Sauce.GG); (c) agent infrastructure and agent-to-agent payments (StitchAI, Agora-mesh, A2A IntentPool, TickPay, AMMO); (d) games (Sparkball, Mission 7).
- Pure infrastructure or dev tooling rarely takes the top spot. Consumer-facing products with a clear user loop, and agent+payments ideas that use Monad's speed, show up most often.

### Gaps
- Moltiverse's 16 winner names, the Madness Seoul winners, the Mission winners, most Blitz winners, and Monad bounty winners at ETHGlobal and other third-party events were not found.

## What judging criteria were published, and what did Monad team members say they were looking for?

### Takeaway
Published criteria are thin. Moltiverse lists agent functionality and innovation, Monad integration, community building (for the token track) and technical substance, and it rewards "weird, powerful" agents. Metropolis publishes submission requirements and a VC-heavy judge list but, on the public page, no weighted rubric. I found no direct quotes from Keone Hon, James Hunsaker or Eunice Giarta about what they look for in submissions.

### Cited Findings
- **Moltiverse** criteria: agent functionality and innovation, integration with Monad, community building (Agent+Token track), and substantial technical achievement, with emphasis on "weird, powerful" agents. Judges: Haseeb (Dragonfly), Frankie (Paradigm), Cookies (Monad), LILMON (Nad.fun) and Patrick Chu (AUSD). The $40K liquidity boost goes to the highest-market-cap token, which makes traction an explicit metric. — [moltiverse.dev](https://moltiverse.dev/)
- **Metropolis** requirements: a working product plus demo, write-up and code, judged per track. Judges include Keone Hon and Eunice Giarta (Monad Foundation), Will Nuelle (Galaxy), Maria Shen (Electric Capital) and Alex Svanevik (Nansen), plus Castle Island, Paradigm, CoinFund, IOSG and others. — [Metropolis](https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **Monad Madness** is judged by panels of VC judges and investors (Paradigm, Pantera and Electric Capital are mentioned as connected investors), and it targets teams that already have an MVP and are fundraising. — [Monad announcement](https://www.monad.xyz/announcements/monads-1m-pitch-competition-monad-madness); [Followin interview summary](https://followin.io/en/feed/16282461)
- **Blitz** is peer-voted by fellow builders. — [Luma](https://luma.com/monad-blitz-ethdenver-2026)
- **evm/accathon** entry was selective ("Applications will be scrutinized thoroughly"). — [Monad Substack](https://monadxyz.substack.com/p/the-first-monad-hackathon-evmaccathon)
- **Nitro** framing (editorial, not a team quote): "consistent execution and product-market fit over hype." — [Monad Pulse #016](https://themonadpulse.substack.com/p/the-monad-pulse-016)

### Inferences
- The judge makeup (VCs and Nansen) and the requirements (a working product and demo) suggest that fundability, a real working demo, and a clear use of Monad's throughput and low latency count for more than novelty alone. This is an inference, not a published rubric.

### Gaps
- No Metropolis scoring rubric or weights were found publicly; they may sit behind the hackathon.monad.xyz login.
- No first-person statements from Keone, James Hunsaker, Eunice or the devrel team on judging priorities were located. X posts and podcasts are the likely sources but were not retrievable.

## Did winners go on to raise money or launch on mainnet?

### Takeaway
There is limited but real evidence. Several Madness Bangkok winners had or raised VC money (Sparkball $2M, Pulse $1.8M seed), and Monad cites EarnOS and RareBet as showcase alumni. Gorillionaire kept shipping after the event. I found no systematic tracking of mainnet launches.

### Cited Findings
- Sparkball: $2M investment from L1D and CMS Holdings, plus a partnership with MON Protocol. The timing relative to Madness is unclear and the money may predate the event. — [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604366660)
- Pulse: a $1.8M seed round with Lemniscap and Collab Currency, with launch targeted for Q1 2025. — [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604366660)
- A Feb 2025 interview said Madness had given out more than $1M in prizes, connected teams with Paradigm, Pantera and Electric Capital, and named EarnOS and RareBet Sports as example winners. — [Followin](https://followin.io/en/feed/16282461)
- Gorillionaire (evm/accathon winner) published a "June wrap-up" of continued development in 2025. — [Gorillionaire Substack](https://luduvigo.substack.com/p/gorillionaire-june-wrap-up)
- Hong Kong winners got a fast track into IOSG's Kickstarter program. — [PANews](https://panews.io/articles/y390l6bi)

### Inferences
- Madness selects teams that are already fundable, so later raises may reflect selection rather than the effect of winning.

### Gaps
- Mainnet launch status of the Madness, evm/accathon, Moltiverse and Rebel winners after Monad mainnet (chain ID 143) was not verified.
- No news-outlet coverage (The Block, CoinDesk, Blockworks, Decrypt) specifically tracking winner outcomes was found.
