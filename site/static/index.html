Beta software. Test money only.

BilbyPay is an early beta built for the Monad Metropolis hackathon. It runs on Monad testnet: every balance, payment and gift card is a test value with no real-world worth, and gift card codes are demos that can't be redeemed.

The smart contracts, server and apps have not been audited and may contain bugs. Don't send real funds, don't reuse real passwords or secrets, and expect accounts or data to be reset. Use at your own risk.
